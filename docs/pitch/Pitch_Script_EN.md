## PRELIMINARY: 2:00 (+1 min Q&A)

**[S1 · 0:00–0:12] Hook**
"Quick question: who here has lived with a bad roommate? *(raise hand)* Your budgets matched, right? But did your lifestyles?"

**[S2 · 0:12–0:27] Problem**
"In Istanbul, listing rents rose 449% in four years. New-tenant rents rose 34.5% in just one year. A roommate is no longer a choice, it's a necessity. But everyone checks the budget, and nobody checks the bedtime."

**[S3 · 0:27–0:42] Before**
"So we built Roomie: a 25-question compatibility test, a match score, and no messages without a mutual like. The app is in App Store review right now. But at 9:30 this morning we had a plain support page, zero social content, and no launch plan."

**[S4 · 0:42–1:12] Manus orchestration**
"Today I made Manus our launch team. With Apify I pulled 200 TikTok videos: listings feel untrustworthy, and 'bad roommate' stories get all the views. Manus combined that with a 51-source market report and built our personas, positioning and cold-start plan. Then it created the brand kit and a 'Red Flags' content series, and with fal.ai we made the visuals and video. One Manus project, one shared context. I made the decisions."

**[S5 → S6 · 1:12–1:40] Live demo**
*(S5: let the video play 3 seconds)* "This is our first post: 'I'll do the dishes later.'"
*(S6)* "And the landing page is live right now, in Turkish and English. Scan the QR and join the waitlist. The form tells us which campus wants Roomie, so that's where we launch first. And every Monday, Manus scans the news and writes that week's post and TikTok scripts on its own."

**[S7 · 1:40–1:55] Before → After**
"In five and a half hours we went from an app waiting for approval to a live launch machine. Our target: a thousand sign-ups in ten weeks. The day Apple approves, the campaign goes live."

**[S8 · 1:55–2:00] Close**
"Roomie: your budgets match, but do your lifestyles? Thank you."

## Likely questions
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What did Manus do vs. you? | "I made the strategic calls: the message, the campus strategy, what to publish. Manus did the research, brand kit, landing page and automation. Apify was the data layer, fal.ai the visual layer." |
| How many users do you have? | "We're pre-launch; the app is in App Store review. We opened the waitlist today: X sign-ups." (Be honest.) |
| Facebook groups and listing sites already exist. Why you? | "They're listing boards. We measure compatibility. No messages without a mutual like, photos are scanned automatically, and every chat starts with a safety note." |
| How will you solve cold start? | "Density before breadth: one campus at a time. We pick it from waitlist data and open when there are enough profiles there." |
| Do you verify identity? | "Not yet. We have email verification, automatic photo scanning, and report/block. We build trust through mechanisms, not promises." |
| Is the match score scientific? | "No, it's a product heuristic. It starts the right conversation; it's not a guarantee." |
| Where do the numbers come from? | "449% is the BETAM/sahibindex listing index. 34.5% is the Central Bank's New Tenant Rent Index. Manus cited every number and flagged what it couldn't verify." |
| Business model? | "Free at launch to build the network. Later: premium features such as boosted profiles and more filters." *(Use your real plan.)* |

## Pronunciation and timing tips
- Say the numbers slowly: "four hundred forty-nine percent", "thirty-four point five percent".
- Practice 3 times with a timer. If you're still on S5 at 1:40, jump straight to S7.
- No internet: everything works except the QR and live counter. Say the link out loud: "app dot roomieapp dot site".
